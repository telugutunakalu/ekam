What is EKAM?

EKAM is a unique cultural event that brings literature and cinema together on a single platform, celebrating storytelling in both written and visual forms. Open to everyone—from book lovers and movie enthusiasts to aspiring creators and professionals—EKAM is designed as an inclusive, all-day experience that blends inspiration, learning, interaction, and entertainment.

The event features:

  • Interactive sessions with prominent personalities from literature and cinema
  • Fusion discussions between authors and filmmakers
  • Book and app launches
  • Hands-on workshops for aspirants
  • Dedicated networking spaces
  • Engaging activities throughout the day

Telugu Collective

A Section 8 Company focused on promoting Telugu literature to the current generation through various platforms. We have been consistently and successfully conducting literary events in the post-COVID period, building a strong and engaged community around Telugu literature. With a growing base of 40,000+ followers on Instagram and a cumulative digital reach exceeding 1 million, we represent one of the largest and most active Telugu reading communities.

@telugu_collective

The Psychic Filmmaker

A knowledge-driven community for filmmakers. Through learning content and live events, we bring filmmakers together and create opportunities. Psychic Filmmaker has been actively conducting large-scale networking events and has established itself as a strong platform within the creative and filmmaking ecosystem. With a robust digital presence of 130,000+ followers on Instagram and a peak reach exceeding 4 million, we have built a highly engaged and vibrant creative community.
